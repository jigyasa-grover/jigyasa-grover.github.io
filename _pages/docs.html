Contract Stability & Error Handling

Versioning, deprecation & error recovery

Endpoints under /api/v1/ follow Semantic Versioning (currently 1.0.0). Additive properties may be introduced without breaking clients; any breaking schema change will be released under /api/v2/ while keeping /api/v1/ live for at least 90 days with a deprecation notice in /openapi.json and on this page.

All endpoints are served statically over HTTPS by GitHub Pages. Missing paths return HTTP 404; agents encountering a 404 should re-discover valid paths from /openapi.json, /api/v1/index.json, or /llms.txt. For collaboration questions, see Engagement Modes & Pricing or Work With Me.
