Bridging production scale with practical clarity

I am a Machine Learning Engineer (Tech Lead, Product Selector Ranking & Engineering Lead, Org-Wide AI Initiative) at Uber in the San Francisco Bay Area. My day-to-day work spans high-throughput Mixture-of-Experts ranking and cross-vertical T5 user representation models serving ~750M to 950M daily requests across 500+ cities, alongside 0→1 internal agentic developer tooling.

Before Uber, I led AI & Research at Bordo AI, built a 0→1 feature store and ads bidding engine at Faire, owned sub-50ms billion-request ads ranking at Twitter (X), and engineered ads signal monitoring at Meta. I hold an M.S. in Computer Science (AI) from UC San Diego and attended the Cornell, Maryland, Max Planck Pre-doctoral Research School (CMMRS) on a full scholarship. (See full career, research & 13+ grants timeline →)

In parallel since 2015, I write, teach, and advise independently through Afterdays (30+ startups, enterprises, and venture funds) and serve on the Google Developer Advisory Board (gDAB) alongside AI/ML GDE recognition. I co-authored Sculpting Data for ML (assigned at Cornell Tech, 100+ citations), was featured on the Google I/O main stage and in Google's Gemini Omni Flash launch, and have trained 50,000+ engineers and leaders across LinkedIn Learning, corporate workshops, and 200+ stages in 21 countries.
