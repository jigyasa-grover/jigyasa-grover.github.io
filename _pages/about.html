At the intersection of production scale, agentic engineering, and technical clarity

Currently, I serve as a Machine Learning Engineer (Tech Lead — Rider Personalization & Ranking) at Uber, leading end-to-end personalization, multi-task Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) ranking, and cross-vertical user representation models serving millions of daily riders across 500+ cities, alongside building 0→1 internal agentic developer tooling.

Over the past decade, my engineering career has focused on taking machine learning and generative AI out of notebooks and into high-throughput, low-latency production environments:

  • Bordo AI — Served as Lead Engineer, AI & Research, architecting a 0→1 provider-agnostic multi-LLM conversational analytics stack (Claude, Gemini, GPT, LangGraph, custom RAG) across 25+ enterprises.
  • Faire — Built a real-time marketplace feature store, calibrated XGBoost ads bidding engine with Bayesian cold-start smoothing, and monetization simulator from 0→1.
  • Twitter (X) — Owned billion-request/day ads click and conversion ranking models under strict sub-50ms SLAs, dynamic feature hydration, and privacy-preserving post-ATT serving infrastructure.
  • Facebook (Meta) — Built statistical anomaly detection systems on live Pixel conversion streams, following earlier computational research at the San Diego Supercomputer Center, IRD Paris, and National Research Council Canada.

On the ecosystem and governance side, I serve on the Google Developer Advisory Board (gDAB) and Google Cloud BI Council, hold recognition as a Google Developer Expert (AI/ML) and World Economic Forum Global Shaper (Palo Alto Hub), and advise founders, product leaders, and investors across 30+ startups, enterprises, and venture funds. In May 2024, I was featured on the Google I/O main keynote stage—introduced by Sundar Pichai—sharing practitioner insights on Gemini 1.5 Pro's 1-million-token context window.

Guided by the belief that we rise by lifting others, I co-authored Sculpting Data for ML: The First Act of Machine Learning (95+ academic citations), authored peer-reviewed Springer chapters on privacy-preserving ML and deepfake detection, teach four courses on LinkedIn Learning—including Hands-On AI: Building Agents with Google ADK and Evaluating AI Agents with Google ADK—and deliver corporate AI training with organizations including Morgan Stanley, FedEx, Accenture, Roblox, Silicon Valley Security Group, and AI Aspire, reaching 50,000+ learners globally.
