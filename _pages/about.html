Bridging production scale with practical clarity

I currently serve as a Machine Learning Engineer (Tech Lead — Rider Personalization & Ranking) at Uber, leading multi-task ranking and user representation models across 500+ cities alongside 0→1 internal agentic developer tooling.

Previously, I led AI & Research at Bordo AI, built a 0→1 feature store and ads bidding engine at Faire, owned sub-50ms billion-request ads ranking at Twitter (X), and interned at Meta after completing my M.S. in Computer Science (AI) at UC San Diego. (See full career & research timeline →)

Beyond my day-to-day engineering work, I serve on the Google Developer Advisory Board (gDAB), hold Google Developer Expert (AI/ML) recognition, and advise 30+ startups and venture funds. I was featured on the Google I/O main keynote stage, co-authored Sculpting Data for ML (95+ citations), and have trained 50,000+ engineers and leaders through LinkedIn Learning and corporate programs with Morgan Stanley, FedEx, Accenture, Roblox, Silicon Valley Security Group, and AI Aspire.
