# Privacy Policy — jigyasa-grover.github.io

`https://jigyasa-grover.github.io/` is a static personal website hosted on GitHub Pages over HTTPS with no user accounts, login walls, third-party ad networks, or data-broker tracking.

- **Analytics:** Aggregate traffic and engagement measurement via Google Analytics 4 (`G-GC9TNKJ6GT`); no contact form content or personally identifiable inquiry details are logged to analytics.
- **Local Preferences:** Optional light/dark theme preference stored locally in `localStorage` (`jg-theme`); aggregate session referrer stored temporarily in `sessionStorage`.
- **Voluntary Inquiries:** The direct contact form prepares an explicit email draft to `grover.jigyasa2@gmail.com`. Bookings made on Intro, Passionfroot, Naano, or 8x are governed by those platforms' respective privacy policies.
- **Contact:** Jigyasa Grover (`grover.jigyasa2@gmail.com`) — https://jigyasa-grover.github.io/work-with-me/
