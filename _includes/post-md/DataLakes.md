**DATA VARIETY** 🍏🍌🍇 There is no schema definition or a structure in a [#DataLake](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataLake) during the capturing stage and therefore raw data from multiple sources can be streamed in real-time into this centralized repository without a need for any [#DataTransformation](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataTransformation).

**IMPLEMENTATION** ☁️💾🔢 Since a [#DataLake](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataLake) can hold 100 terabytes or even petabytes of majorly enterprise data, they are often implemented in cloud-based, distributed storage systems.

**VALUE ADDITION** ⏳⚱️🤓 Data is the food for learning algorithms, and a [#DataLake](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataLake) provides the perfect solution to harness the knowledge from data across multiple resources, in a lesser time frame leading to in-depth analysis, involved collaboration, and rapid decision making.

**ALL IN ONE** 🤖🥇👀 Especially with [#MachineLearning](https://twitter.com/hashtag/MachineLearning), a [#DataLake](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataLake) provides a one-stop solution for enterprises to use historical data, hypothesize, generate insights, refine assumptions, and assess results without needing to go outside the system.

**BENEFITS** 🎟🎞📝 Besides simplifying [#DataManagement](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataManagement) across structured, semi-structured, and unstructured formats, like texts, images, SQL tables, etc. a [#DataLake](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataLake) helps lower the cost of ownership, speeds up [#DataAnalytics](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataAnalytics), & provides a solid foundation for [#BusinessIntelligence](https://twitter.com/hashtag/BusinessIntelligence).

**CHALLENGES** 🧩🤸🏻‍♀️🔐 Because of the big data variety in [#DataLake](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataLake), it is important to develop mechanisms to catalog/tag data to make it easily discoverable. Additionally, for security/privacy & [#DataGovernance](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataGovernance) access controls, network security, and encryptions should be invested in.

**EXAMPLES** 🐠🎯🔢 Microsoft [@Azure](https://twitter.com/Azure), Amazon [@awscloud](https://twitter.com/awscloud), [@qubole](https://twitter.com/qubole), [@Informatica](https://twitter.com/Informatica), etc. provide some of the popular [#DataLake](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataLake) solutions, each with its own set of prominent features, and one can choose based on their specific use case.
