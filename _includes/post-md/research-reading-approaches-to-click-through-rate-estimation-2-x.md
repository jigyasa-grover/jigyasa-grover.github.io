Continuing [part one](https://jigyasa-grover.github.io/research-reading-approaches-to-click-through-rate-estimation-1-x/) of this segment, I will share yet another research reading specifically related to click-through rate (CTR) estimation viz. [Deeply Supervised Semantic Model for Click-Through Rate Prediction in Sponsored Search](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.10739.pdf) by *Yahoo! Research* and *Temple University* published in March 2018.

According to [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2615875?hl=en), ***CTR is a ratio showing how often people who see your ad end up clicking it**. It can be used to gauge how well your keywords and ads are performing.*
