**TROUBLEMAKER** 😈🙅🏻‍♀️😼 Often the issues originate early in [#ML](https://twitter.com/hashtag/ML) system’s life cycle, for example at the stage where we collect or annotate the data. These seemingly small issues grow larger into complex challenges affecting the [#MachineLearning](https://twitter.com/hashtag/MachineLearning) model development and deployment.

**DETECT ‘EM** 🕵🏻‍♀️👀🤓 The diagnosis of [#DataCascades](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataCascades) is a tough one, especially since there are no clear indicators, tools, to detect them or well-defined metrics to measure their long term effects. We can and should prioritize data in an [#ML](https://twitter.com/hashtag/ML) system along with model development.

**DATA FIRST** 🎖🎁🏆  Efforts towards bringing empiricism in data should be incentivized in the organization (and [#MachineLearning](https://twitter.com/hashtag/MachineLearning) community as a whole). This means recognizing work on rewarding dataset collection, labeling, cleaning, or maintenance, as much as any modeling work.

**MONITOR YOUR DATA **👀📈🔬 Similar to developing good model performance metrics, we should develop metrics to measure health & goodness of data too! Metrics related to data distribution, feature coverage, data freshness, etc. can be used to measure/identify [#DataCascades](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataCascades) early on.

**SCULPT IT RIGHT** 🧱⏳📖 To avoid [#DataCascading](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DataCascading), get started right by fostering data literacy for [#MachineLearning](https://twitter.com/hashtag/MachineLearning). Read through [@DataForML](https://twitter.com/DataForML) and learn how to create your very own quality dataset using [#Python](https://twitter.com/hashtag/Python) and other [#OpenSource](https://twitter.com/hashtag/OpenSource) tools!
