In the last week of 2022, I was having a conversation with [Irene Lyakovetsky](https://www.linkedin.com/in/irenelyakovetsky/) from [SaugaTalks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/saugatuckworldwide/) about how incredible of a year it was with some really cool AI applications like ChatGPT, DALL-E 2, PaLM, and so many more.

As we discussed if AI can be sentient and would AI-powered movies be that enjoyable, we drew out a list of the top 5 trends we are excited about in the upcoming year 👀

📌 **Generative AI** that mimics creativity, one of the most unique skills humans possess.

📌 Comprehensive **Data Protection Laws and Regulations** as curation and consumption of native/synthetic data amplifies with a focus on data-centric approaches to AI.

📌 **Explainable AI** to steer clear of irreversible damages that can be caused by applications of AI in risk-prone fields like healthcare, defense, and legal.

📌 **Metaverse** with its mind-boggling promise to transcend the physical layer and provide a ‘feeling of physical presence’ despite being in a virtual world.

📌 More sophistication in **Low-Code/No-Code Solutions** to boost the adoption of AI in multifarious domains and help build an MVP quickly.
