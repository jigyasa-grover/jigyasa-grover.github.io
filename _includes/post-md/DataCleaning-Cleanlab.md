Did you know that the ML benchmark datasets we heavily rely on like MNIST, ImageNet, CIFAR, etc., can have thousands of errors in their labels? 😱 Check out [labelerrors.com](http://labelerrors.com/) for a few examples.

Last week, I attended [Snorkel AI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/snorkel-ai/)'s conference on [#DataCentricAI](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=datacentricai&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A6961212413765439488) - where one of my favorite talks was ***[Cleanlab](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cleanlab/): AI for Correcting Errors in Any Dataset***!

It was fascinating to know that what started as a graduate school project from an idea of finding an erroneous label in a benchmark dataset - of using [#ConfidentLearning](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=confidentlearning&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A6961212413765439488) to find and fix label errors in ML datasets, is now being used to correct data at a large scale by companies like Microsoft, Tesla, and Google is fab 👏🏻

Also, [cleanlab.ai](http://cleanlab.ai/) is [#OpenSource](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=opensource&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A6961212413765439488), no-code, and an automated solution!
