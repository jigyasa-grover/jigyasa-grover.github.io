<p>Hiya Fellas 😀</p>
<p>So, while I was browsing through various developments in Pharo, I came across a very eye-catching paper on <em><strong>Bootstrapping Reflective Systems: The case of Pharo</strong></em> .</p>
<blockquote><p>For beginners, Bootstrapping is basically the process of writing a compiler or an assembler in the source programming language in which it is intended to compile. Applying this technique leads to self-hosting compiler that proceeds without external input.</p></blockquote>
<p>Though, I am still a newbie and in the process of grasping all this ingenious stuff.<br />
But I am sure that this paper ( <a href="https://jigyasagrover.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/poli12-bootstrappingsmalltalk-scp.pdf">Poli12-BootstrappingSmalltalk-SCP</a> ) by <em>G. Polito , S. Ducasse , L. Fabresse , N. Bouraqadi , B. van Ryseghem </em>would make for an interesting read.</p>
<p>Do scan through it and post below your ideas and queries !</p>
