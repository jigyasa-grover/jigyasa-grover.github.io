<p>Excitement was super high, yet again.</p>
<p>Reading the words <strong>&#8220;Congratulations Jigyasa ! Your proposal is now a GSoC 2016 project! &#8220;</strong> on the brand new &amp; shiny <a href="https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/">summerofcode.withgoogle.com</a> on a late Friday night pumped up the heart which was already beating fast enough.</p>
<p>As is the ritual, the first thing I did was call my sister &amp; parents at ~12:30am, who have been my support throughout which was followed by thanking the organization members and congratulating fellow successful participants.</p>
<p>There is a sense of happiness and pride as I covenant to take up on another exciting project this summer. I have been working with <strong>FOSSASIA</strong> for more than 1.5 years now and it has been a lovely journey so far. I have had help from amazing mentors, interacted with team members from around the globe virtually as well as at the <strong>FOSSASIA OpenTech Summit</strong> that it feels like family to me.</p>
<p>This time I would be working with <em>Michael Peter Christen</em>, the maker of <a href="http://yacy.net/">YaCy</a> and <a href="http://loklak.org/">Loklak</a>. Google Summer of Code 2016 will be spent with exploring the Loklak API and tinkering with it to develop a great product.</p>
<p>Looking forward to an amazing learning experience and a fruitful summer ahead.<br />
Ciao !</p>
