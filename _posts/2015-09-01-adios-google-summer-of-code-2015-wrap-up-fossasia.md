<p><em>Summer 2015</em> flew by too quickly. My heartiest gratitude to <strong>Google Summer of Code 2015</strong> and <strong>FOSSASIA</strong> for making the past couple of months the most memorable ones.</p>
<p>I feel blessed to have been dispensed with an opportunity of this level.<br />
Participating and successfully completing Google Summer of Code 2015 with<strong> </strong>the given task of developing <em>searchQuick: An offline text search utility in Pharo</em> would not have been possible without the ceaseless support of my mentoring organization FOSSASIA, its amiable admins <em>Mario Behling</em> and <em>Hong</em> <em>Phuc</em>, my kind(est) and most accommodating mentors <em>Martin Bähr</em> and <em>Sean DeNigris</em>.<br />
No doubt in the fact that Pharo has one of the most helpful community out there, my sincerest thanks to <em>Max Leske</em>, <em>Kilon Alios</em> and other for assisting in every way possible.</p>
<p>As I wrap-up with this program I acknowledge learning loads of cool stuff right from using VCS day-in day-out to opening issues, reviewing bugs, being active on forums &amp; mailing lists, grasping UI/UX and OO coding techniques, maintaining documentation stuff, blogging &amp; tweeting &amp; chatting on IRC (on social fronts 😉 ), etc etc, it has been indeed a great experience !</p>
<p>And I take an oath to augment my new-found-happiness-means by continuing to contribute (even tit-bits) to this exquisite Open Source community.</p>
<p>Looking forward to many more such happenings, till then * fork, explore, contribute! *</p>
<p>Cheers !</p>
