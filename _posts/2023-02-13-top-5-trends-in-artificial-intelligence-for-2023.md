<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the last week of 2022, I was having a conversation with <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/irenelyakovetsky/">Irene Lyakovetsky</a> from <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/saugatuckworldwide/">SaugaTalks</a> about how incredible of a year it was with some really cool AI applications like ChatGPT, DALL-E 2, PaLM, and so many more.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As we discussed if AI can be sentient and would AI-powered movies be that enjoyable, we drew out a list of the top 5 trends we are excited about in the upcoming year 👀</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">📌 <strong>Generative AI</strong> that mimics creativity, one of the most unique skills humans possess.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">📌 Comprehensive <strong>Data Protection Laws and Regulations</strong> as curation and consumption of native/synthetic data amplifies with a focus on data-centric approaches to AI.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">📌 <strong>Explainable AI</strong> to steer clear of irreversible damages that can be caused by applications of AI in risk-prone fields like healthcare, defense, and legal.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">📌 <strong>Metaverse</strong> with its mind-boggling promise to transcend the physical layer and provide a ‘feeling of physical presence’ despite being in a virtual world.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">📌 More sophistication in <strong>Low-Code/No-Code Solutions</strong> to boost the adoption of AI in multifarious domains and help build an MVP quickly.</p>
