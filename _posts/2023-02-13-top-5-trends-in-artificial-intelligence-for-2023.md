<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the last week of 2022, I was having a conversation with <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/irenelyakovetsky/">Irene Lyakovetsky</a> from <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/saugatuckworldwide/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_detail_base%3BeqNbktooTCm1J0jks%2F9xnQ%3D%3D">SaugaTalks</a> about how incredible of a year it was with some really cool AI applications like Chat GPT, DALL E V2, PaLM, and so many more.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As we discussed if AI can be sentient and would AI-powered movies be that enjoyable, we drew out a list of the top 5 trends we are excited about in the upcoming year  👀</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">📌 𝗚𝗲𝗻𝗲𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗔𝗜 that mimics creativity, one of the most unique skills humans possess.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">📌 Comprehensive 𝗗𝗮𝘁𝗮 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝘁𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗟𝗮𝘄𝘀 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗥𝗲𝗴𝘂𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀 as curation and consumption of native/synthetic data amplifies with a focus on data-centric approaches to AI.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">📌 𝗘𝘅𝗽𝗹𝗮𝗶𝗻𝗮𝗯𝗹𝗲 𝗔𝗜 to steer clear of irreversible damages that can be caused by applications of AI risk-prone fields like healthcare, defense, and legal.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">📌 𝗠𝗲𝘁𝗮𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗲 with its mind-boggling promise to transcend the physical layer and provide a ‘feeling of physical presence’ despite being in a virtual world.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">📌 More sophistication in 𝗟𝗼𝘄-𝗖𝗼𝗱𝗲/𝗡𝗼-𝗖𝗼𝗱𝗲 𝗦𝗼𝗹𝘂𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀 to boost the adoption of AI in multifarious domains and help build an MVP quickly.</p>
